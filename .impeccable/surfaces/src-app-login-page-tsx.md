Surface: P2-M3 shared login and neutral authenticated account proof.
Audience: DFCAMCLP applicants, students, faculty, and staff testing authentication.
Direction: calm, restrained institutional utility; extend the provisional warm-white, deep-green, grey-border foundation without introducing or implying a finished visual system.
Structure: single shared sign-in panel with portal context, email, password, safe errors, and one primary action; account page is a compact server-protected identity/session proof.
Interaction: explicit labels, keyboard operation, visible focus, password visibility control, browser autocomplete, pending submission state, and generic credential failure.
Responsive: one readable column from 320px phones through desktop; no horizontal overflow or fixed-height card.
Boundaries: portal choice exists only as local form context, is ignored by authentication, and never controls access; no portal shell, membership, role, or permission UI.
Avoid: gradients, glass effects, decorative dashboards, invented branding, hidden labels, role claims, or portal-specific redirects.
