## Decision rules

**A — GLOBAL PROJECT DISCLOSURE:** affiliation, general fictional-data and general reset statements move to first entry and information pages. Remove repeated body/preamble prose only after the new system and persistent access pass FD8 acceptance.

**B — CONTEXTUAL ACTION WARNING:** keep a warning if removal could imply a real submission, official document/policy, delivered message, durable edit, actual appointment, live metric or a relationship between unrelated account/domain identities. Mixed notices lose only their A clause and retain their B meaning near the relevant task. A `DemoNotice` component does not automatically mean A.

Retain truthful demo verbs, sample counts, empty states, accessibility names, fixture contents, status vocabulary and project metadata. These are orientation/data, not interchangeable banners. Do not edit fixtures, authorization/navigation catalogs or technical services to reduce prose. The global default label “Demo workspace” should cease appearing in retained contextual warnings; use task names instead.
